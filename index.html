The Studio

You walk in. Your station is waiting.

The coach tells you where you're lifting today. You grab your band and hip thrust pad from the Cheek Bar, walk to your bench, and the music starts. Everyone begins together. Nobody wanders the floor looking for a free rack.

The Cheek Method™

Six blocks. Every class. Every time.

Designed to build stronger glutes, stronger bodies and stronger confidence. The structure never changes — only the lift inside each block does. By your second visit you already know the shape of the room. That's the point.

01

Activate

3–5 min

Banded lateral walks and glute bridges. The same two moves every class.

02

Build

12–18 min

One heavy compound: hip thrust, squat or RDL. The whole room lifts together.

03

Strength

8–10 min

A second compound — split squat, step-up or leg press.

04

Sculpt

5–18 min

Banded isolation: kickbacks, abduction, back extensions.

05

Burn

5–15 min

A banded burnout ladder. The reps change; the format doesn't.

06

Finish

3–5 min

Stretch, then the closing ritual. Everyone leaves together.

Your First Month

From never touched a barbell to counting your own reps.

01

Cheek Start

Before any regular class, you get a coach to yourself. Learn your station, walk through the Method, set a goal, take optional measurements. No workout pressure.

02

Your first Build

You'll be given a station and a starting weight. The coach demos, counts, and corrects. You'll leave knowing exactly what you lifted.

03

Week two, it clicks

You already know the six blocks. The lift changes, the room doesn't. This is where people stop being nervous and start adding weight.

04

Your first Build Check™

A sit-down with your coach: what's gone up, what's next, what to focus on. Progress you can actually see written down.
