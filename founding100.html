The Cheek Method™

Six blocks. Every class. Every time.

By your second visit you already know the shape of the room. That's what lets you stop thinking about what's next and start thinking about the weight.

01

Activate

3–5 min

Banded walks and glute bridges. The same two moves every class.

02

Build

12–18 min

One heavy compound: hip thrust, squat or RDL. The room lifts together.

03

Strength

8–10 min

A second compound — split squat, step-up or leg press.

04

Sculpt

5–18 min

Banded isolation: kickbacks, abduction, back extensions.

05

Burn

5–15 min

A banded burnout ladder. The reps change; the format doesn't.

06

Finish

3–5 min

Stretch, then the closing ritual. Everyone leaves together.

Questions people actually ask

Before you put your name down.

Am I committing to anything?

No. There's no payment and no obligation. You're on a list, you hear things first, and you decide when there's an actual studio to walk into.

I've never lifted heavy. Is this for me?

Especially. Every member starts with Cheek Start — a one-on-one with a coach, no workout pressure, where you learn your station and where to begin.

Every class has a beginner level, and nobody adds weight until their form is clean.

Is this a weight-loss thing?

No. Nobody here will talk about burning off what you ate. The number we care about is the one on the bar.

I'm postpartum.

Core Values is built for exactly this — pelvic floor activation, deep core work, diastasis-safe movement, taught by coaches with a separate certification for it. Get clearance from your provider, then start there.

What will it cost?

Memberships run from $109/month for four classes to $279/month for everything, with drop-ins at $35. Founders lock their rate for as long as they stay.

When does it open?

We're securing the space now, and pop-up classes run before the studio doors open. This list hears both first.

What do we build?

Strength. Confidence. Cheeks.
