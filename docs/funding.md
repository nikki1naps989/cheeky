## Context

Cheek Club — boutique booty gym, Las Vegas. Twelve stations, 55-minute coach-led
classes, capped at 12 per class.

Membership pricing as published on the site: $109 / $169 / $219 / $279 per month,
drop-ins $35. Founding 100 members lock their rate for as long as they stay, which
is a deliberate trade — early cash and a full room at launch, against permanently
discounted revenue from the first hundred members. Worth modelling that drag
explicitly in the financial model, since it never expires.
